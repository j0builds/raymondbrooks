Services

Eleven ways we work together.

  1. Photography & Videography

    Professional photography and video production for brands, businesses, athletes, creators, and special events. Through our creative network we connect clients with photographers and videographers that fit their specific needs.

  2. Video Editing

    Transform raw footage into engaging content designed to increase attention, engagement, and impact across social media and digital platforms.

  3. Free Creative Consultation

    Meet one-on-one to discuss your goals, content strategy, creative challenges, marketing opportunities, and next steps. Every consultation includes actionable recommendations tailored to your vision.

  4. On-Camera Performance

    Develop confidence, presence, communication skills, and authenticity in front of the camera for content, interviews, public speaking, and personal branding.

  5. Podcasting Workshop

    Learn podcast planning, interviewing technique, audience engagement, recording fundamentals, and content development to launch or improve your podcast.

  6. Storytelling & Leadership

    Discover how storytelling inspires action, builds stronger teams, communicates vision, and creates lasting impact. Craft narratives that connect with audiences and motivate others.

  7. Super Communicators

    Master practical, emotional, and social communication through active listening, strategic questioning, and audience awareness. Strengthen your ability to connect, lead, and influence.

  8. Data & Decision Making

    Analyze performance, identify opportunities, and make informed decisions that support creative and business growth.

  9. Creator Mentorship Program

    A guided development experience for aspiring creators and entrepreneurs seeking accountability, coaching, feedback, networking, and personalized growth strategies.

  10. Aura Farm

    Intentionally develop and project your energy, presence, confidence, and influence. Self-awareness, interpersonal connection, mindset, and creating positive experiences for yourself and those around you.

  11. Creative Network

    Need a photographer, videographer, editor, host, creative strategist, or content creator? 42MEDIA connects clients with trusted creatives who bring ideas to life.
