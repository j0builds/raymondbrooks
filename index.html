Mission & Community Impact

Creating More Than Content.

At 42MEDIA, we believe creativity has the power to change lives.

Through our partnership with Ethos Digital Media Lounge, every service we provide helps support something greater than the project itself. Your fees help create opportunities for individuals with behavioral and developmental disabilities by supporting programs, creative resources, educational experiences, and services designed to fuel their success.

When you choose 42MEDIA, you're not simply investing in media. You're investing in people, potential, and a community where creativity creates opportunity.

How do consultations work?

Every creative consultation is free and by appointment only. We meet one-on-one to discuss your goals, content strategy, creative challenges, and next steps — and you leave with actionable recommendations tailored to your vision.

Where are you located?

We're based at the Ethos Digital Media Lounge, 315 Spring Garden St, Unit 1D, Greensboro, NC 27401.

What are your business hours?

Monday–Friday 9:00 AM–6:00 PM and Saturday 10:00 AM–4:00 PM. All consultations and sessions are by appointment only, so please reach out to book a time.

What if you're unavailable for my project?

42MEDIA is a creative network. If Raymond is unavailable, you can still work with trusted photographers, videographers, editors, designers, and other creatives through the 42MEDIA network.

How does the Ethos Digital Media Lounge partnership work?

Through our partnership with Ethos Digital Media Lounge, your fees help create opportunities for individuals with behavioral and developmental disabilities — supporting programs, creative resources, and educational experiences designed to fuel their success.
